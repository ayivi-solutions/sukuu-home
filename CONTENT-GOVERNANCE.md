# Public content and trade-secret boundary

## Approved public layer

The website may communicate:

- “24 capabilities. One connected system.” as approved in the presentation
- The six presentation clusters: Foundation; Academic Execution; People; Finance; Operations; Engagement & Insight
- The enquiry-to-graduation learner journey
- Mobile-first, offline-capable, low-bandwidth and Ghana-ready commitments
- Human authority, role-appropriate access, approvals and accountable history
- The four-product Sukuu ecosystem and the explainable-support role of the Sukuu Intelligence Bridge
- AYIVI Systems Limited’s identity, ownership, establishment year, reach and service categories

## Keep out of public pages

- Database schemas, table counts, entity relationships and tenancy implementation
- Internal module names and the complete module catalogue
- Source code, infrastructure diagrams, provider credentials and security configuration
- Detailed permission matrices, exception logic and proprietary workflow definitions
- Model, algorithm or recommendation-engine internals
- Internal pricing assumptions, forecasts, margins and build schedules
- Confidential specifications, acceptance criteria and unreleased roadmap commitments

## Copy standard

Lead with school outcomes, explain authority boundaries clearly and avoid unverified absolutes. Product changes must be checked against the current approved functional baseline before the website is updated.
