Procurement questions

What schools usually ask before a demonstration

We use discovery to confirm fit, readiness, scope and the right rollout sequence for each institution.

What is Sukuu ERP?+

A connected institutional operating system that helps schools manage academic work, people, finance, daily operations, communication and leadership insight from one governed environment.

Is Sukuu designed for Ghanaian schools?+

Yes. Sukuu is mobile-first, low-bandwidth conscious, offline-capable for critical work and designed around Ghanaian academic, currency, payment and operating realities.

Does it support different school types and campuses?+

Sukuu is configured around the institution. Discovery confirms campuses, departments, academic structures, roles, policies and the capabilities to activate first.

How is implementation handled?+

Through five controlled stages: discover, configure, migrate, pilot and scale. The priority is to prove the most important school journeys before expanding.

Does Sukuu make decisions for the school?+

No. Sukuu provides authorised evidence, explainable prompts and recommendations. Institutional, safeguarding, academic, financial and employment authority remains with accountable people.
